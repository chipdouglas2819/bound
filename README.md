# BOUND

A mobile-first dodge roguelike. Survive 30 seconds inside the bounding circle.

▶ **Play:** https://chipdouglas2819.github.io/bound/

## What it is

Touch joystick or gyro tilt. Enemy circles spawn and home toward you. You dodge.
Combos slow enemies. Hits drop you one combo tier. The arena tightens as the
clock runs down.

Score buys stat upgrades. Essence buys passives — three equipped at a time.
At Prestige 6 and 12 you unlock 60s and 90s wave-based modes. At Prestige 9
you choose a per-run modifier before each run.

## Tech

Single HTML file. Canvas 2D. No build step. No framework.
Open `index.html` in any modern browser.
